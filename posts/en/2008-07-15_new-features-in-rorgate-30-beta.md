There are three new features in RoRGate 3.0:[^2026-version]

[^2026-version]: That's 0.3, not 3.0, as the screenshots and the previous post say.

## Pictures as icons

![RoRGate picture-as-icon screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juanger/rorgate/master/meta/images/2008-07-15/RoRgate-0.3b.png "Now you can use png and jpeg files as icons")

## Web Inspector[^2026m-inspector]

[^2026m-inspector]: The same Web Inspector ships in every Safari now, and since macOS 13.3 a WKWebView app has to opt in with `isInspectable`.

![RoRGate WebKit inspector screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juanger/rorgate/master/meta/images/2008-07-15/Inspector.png "The WebKit inspector can be accessed through a contextual menu.")

## Preferences

![RoRGate preferences window](/images/2008-07-15_new-features-in-rorgate-30-beta/Preferences.png "Now you can change the name, port and icon of an existing app.")
